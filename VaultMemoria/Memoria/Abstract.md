# Abstract

This work tries to make a 2D multiplatform mobile video game using the Godot open-source engine, with version 4.5.1. The entire development process will follow an agile methodology based on iterations. This will cover from the initial planning and design, the configuration of the project and programming in Godot, the export to the Android and iOS platforms, as well as the distribution of the game in the corresponding application stores. The genre of the game will be a hybrid between idle and task tracking with a musical aesthetic focused on the piano. Idle games have waiting as their main mechanic, so combining it with a productivity part results in taking advantage of time and gamifying the tasks that are done. Taking advantage of the characteristics of mobile phones, the use of notifications will be incorporated, and the life usage of the application will be controlled for task tracking mode. And taking advantage of the functionalities of the app stores, the synchronization of the user's account in the store will be added with achievements that can be achieved in the game. To top off the work, tests will also be carried out with users to obtain feedback and improve the product.
